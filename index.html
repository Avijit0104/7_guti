সাত গুটি Sat Guti · Seven Stones

Break the stack. Rebuild it before the ball finds you.

Players per team

How to play
  • Attackers take turns throwing the ball at the stack of 7 stones. The power bar swings: release at the strength you want.
  • A soft hit knocks off only the top stones. A hard, low hit sends all 7 flying.
  • When the stack breaks, attackers must rebuild it: biggest stone first.
  • Defenders grab the ball and throw it at attackers. A direct hit puts that player out.
  • Hay, wells, pots and trees block the ball. Runners can hide behind them.
  • Rebuilding scores 1 point (1–3 stones knocked), 2 points (4–6) or 3 points (all 7). Stopping the attackers scores 2.
  • First team to 7 points wins.
